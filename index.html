About Us

Built by a creator,
for creators.

I started as an online graphic designer. I grew an audience of over half a million followers — people who trusted my taste, my work, and my voice. Then I asked myself: what if I built something for them?

So I did. I built an app, launched it through my social channels, and sold over 10,000 licenses. My audience was already there — and the tool gave them something they actually wanted.

That experience taught me something most creators haven't discovered yet: the right software product can scale in a way that no course, merch line, or brand deal ever will. A tool works for you around the clock. It compounds. It grows with your audience.

I started Ghostcode because I want other creators to experience that. You've already done the hard part — building the trust. We'll handle the rest.
